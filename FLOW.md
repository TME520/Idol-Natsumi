- Baseline: `update-19-2` at `a1f0e2d` (includes NT-01, NT-02, NF-01, NC-01, NC-02, NC-03, NC-11).
- File: `idol_natsumi/idol_natsumi.ino`. `lab/` ignored. No gameplay code was changed.
- Line numbers are from that commit and will drift.
- Series: `NFLOW-xx`. Do not renumber. Status of every item below is **Open**.
- Already fixed and not re-opened: NC-01, NC-02, NC-03, NC-11, NT-01, NT-02, NF-01. Cooking notes NC-04..NC-10 were left alone; nothing in that list is required to explain a stuck screen below.

## Golden path

A normal session is one home day-loop with rides only when the destination tile differs from `currentTile`.

1. **Boot.** `VERSION_SCREEN` (text) → `M5_SCREEN` → `MOTTO_SCREEN` → `TITLE_SCREEN2`. Each beat is `changeState(..., microWait)` (60 frames, about 3 s). The title menu waits.
2. **New or continue.** `0` / Enter on "NEW GAME" calls `resetGameState()` (age 11, tile `HOME`, empty delivery flag) and drops straight into `HOME_LOOP`. Continue loads the save, or the same reset if there is no file, then also `HOME_LOOP`. Intro (`2`) is a dialog chain back to the title and does not start a save (`gameStarted` stays false).
3. **Home.** `HOME_LOOP` is a ROOM. TAB or the number row opens Stats, Inventory, Food, Training, Competition, Health, Rest, Garden, Events, Cards. Space opens Challenges. Energy 0 blocks Food and the outing menus with "Natsumi is too tired". Rest stays available, so a tired day is not a lock.
4. **Food.** The Food menu is home UI. Fridge / Cook (`FOOD_COOK`) and Orderibi (`FOOD_ORDER`) are the HOME tile. Restaurant (`FOOD_REST`, age 15+) is CITY. Conbimart is supposed to be OUTSKIRTS (see NFLOW-03). Neko Cafe is CITY in the current switch. A ride plays only when `targetTile != currentTile`, then the shop front, then the activity.
5. **Cook.** Arrows move the fridge grid, Enter toggles an ingredient, Space cooks. A real recipe sets hunger, shows `FOOD_COOK2` for `microWait`, then `ACTION_OUTCOME`, then the Food menu.
6. **Order.** Pick a dish → pay → `FOOD_ORDER6` sets `waitingForFoodDelivery` and returns home. Ten 5-second pulses later (about 50 s), if Natsumi is on `HOME_LOOP`, an idle slideshow, or a top-level menu and no delayed transition is counting, the game enters `FOOD_ORDER7` → `FOOD_ORDER8` → outcome → `FOOD_ORDER9` → outcome → home. That chain matches the NF-01 fix.
7. **Train / compete / health / rest / events.** Each outing is the same shape: menu → (ride if the tile changed) → activity → debrief or `ACTION_OUTCOME` → menu or home. Sleep and the onsen recover energy on the 5-second pulse. Meditation is a 5-minute timer. The library is 30×5 s. A competition run is 50 notes.
8. **Idle at home.** With the home menu closed and no key down, `counterToScreensaver` climbs once per 5-second pulse. Past 10 (about 55 s) a visitor knocks, or the tutorial/stat slideshow (`IDLE_HOME`) starts. Any key returns home on the next pulse. A food order in flight suppresses visitors until the delivery flag clears.

## What would make the game feel broken

1. **NFLOW-01.** Starting sing, dance, swim, gym, run, a competition countdown, the library, or meditation and tapping again sends Natsumi home. That is the ordinary "did it start?" press.
2. **NFLOW-02.** A competition run is a trap until 50 notes are caught. Combined with NFLOW-01, the countdown before it can also dump the player home.
3. **NFLOW-03 and NFLOW-04.** Rides disagree with the map the player is learning: Conbimart is not the outskirts, and the Food menu forgets that Natsumi went out. The next trip is missing or plays at the wrong time, and the ride-home energy step lands on Cook or Orderibi.
4. **NFLOW-05.** A paid cafe visit either kicks the player home on the first extra key, or sits for minutes with the Food menu still live.
5. **NFLOW-06.** Less common, but a mash can skip a charge (free food) or a visitor gift. It feels like the button did the wrong scene.
6. **NFLOW-07, NFLOW-08, NFLOW-09, NFLOW-10.** Dead ESC, leftover ghost-key handling, an 8 s shower, and a held key blocking visitors. Annoying, and they do not by themselves stop a careful playthrough.

## Checked, not filed as new bugs

- **Boot → title → new/continue → `HOME_LOOP`.** The auto beats use one pending `changeState` target each. New game resets the tile, delivery flag, and menus. Continue does not resume a mid-activity state (`continueStateLoaded` is never set; that is the NB-L10 choice).
- **Food delivery `FOOD_ORDER6` → wait → `FOOD_ORDER7`–`9`.** The flag is set once, the doorbell retries until home or a top-level menu is idle, `FOOD_ORDER7` turns the home menu off, and `FOOD_ORDER8`/`9` plus the two outcome steps use `foodDeliveryKeyEdge()` instead of `keyList()[0]`. NF-01 holds. The doorbell does wait out garden, sleep, meditation, and minigames; it is not dropped.
- **Cook / Space.** `FOOD_COOK` disables the menu and is excluded from the any-key dismiss, so Space reaches `cookFood()`. NC-01 / NC-02 hold.
- **Screensaver while the home menu is open.** The pulse holds `counterToScreensaver` at 0 while `menuOpened` (NB-M08). A key also clears it.
- **`REST_MEDITATE2`.** Nothing calls `changeState` into it. Sleep does use `REST_SLEEP2`, and that dialog is dismissed by the overlay. Not a trap.
- **Shower, sleep, onsen timers.** They are driven by `millis()` or the 5-second pulse and do reach their end state. The shower's missing ESC is NFLOW-09; the timers themselves fire.
